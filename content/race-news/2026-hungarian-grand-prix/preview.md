### Background

The Hungarian Grand Prix takes place at the Hungaroring in Mogyoród, marking the 41st time the circuit has hosted a Formula One World Championship round. The event is the eleventh round of the 2026 Formula One World Championship and the 41st running of the Hungarian Grand Prix as a championship event. The race weekend runs from 24 to 26 July.

### Previous race

At the Belgian Grand Prix on 19 July 2026, Andrea Kimi Antonelli claimed victory after 44 laps under sunny skies. Charles Leclerc crossed the line in second place, with Max Verstappen completing the podium. Lewis Hamilton finished fourth and Oscar Piastri rounded out the top five.

### Championship standings

Antonelli leads the Drivers' Championship with 204 points, having extended his advantage with the Belgian Grand Prix win. Lewis Hamilton sits second on 159 points, with George Russell close behind on 154 points. Charles Leclerc is fourth on 126 points following his Belgian podium, while Lando Norris is fifth on 103 points. Mercedes dominate the Constructors' Championship on 358 points, well clear of Ferrari on 285 points. McLaren are third on 195 points, Red Bull fourth on 151 points, and Alpine fifth on 61 points.

### Practice

Three free practice sessions were held ahead of the race. Charles Leclerc topped the opening session on Friday, with Max Verstappen second and Lewis Hamilton third, though Leclerc suffered a mechanical problem during the session. Hamilton went fastest in the second session later that afternoon, ahead of Leclerc and Lando Norris. In Saturday's final practice, Norris edged out Hamilton and Antonelli in what was described as a tight session, suggesting a competitive field heading into the race.
