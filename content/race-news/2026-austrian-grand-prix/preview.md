### The race
The 2026 Austrian Grand Prix is the eighth round of the 2026 Formula One World Championship, held at the Red Bull Ring in Spielberg, Styria. It marks the 39th time the Austrian Grand Prix has featured as a round of the World Championship, and the twenty-second time the Red Bull Ring circuit has hosted a Formula One event, a tally that includes two editions of the Styrian Grand Prix.

### Championship standings
Mercedes lead the Constructors' Championship with 262 points, a commanding margin over Ferrari on 190 and McLaren on 141. Red Bull sit fourth on 89 points, with Alpine fifth on 60. In the Drivers' Championship, Mercedes junior Andrea Kimi Antonelli holds the lead on 156 points, with teammate George Russell third on 106. Lewis Hamilton, now racing for Ferrari, sits second on 115 points, keeping the top three tightly contested within the same two teams. Charles Leclerc is fourth on 75 points and Lando Norris fifth on 73, meaning the battle for the lower points positions remains close heading into Austria.

### Previous race
At the Barcelona-Catalunya Grand Prix on 14 June 2026, Lewis Hamilton took victory for Ferrari across 66 laps under sunny skies. George Russell crossed the line second, with Lando Norris completing the podium in third. Max Verstappen finished fourth and Oscar Piastri fifth, rounding out a competitive top five.

### Practice
Three free practice sessions took place ahead of the Austrian Grand Prix. Kimi Antonelli set the pace in both the first and second sessions, with George Russell and Oscar Piastri among those closest to him. A red flag interrupted the closing stages of the opening session after Sergio Pérez stopped on track in his Cadillac. In the third and final practice session, Russell went quickest ahead of Antonelli, with Lewis Hamilton third.
