### The race

The 2026 Dutch Grand Prix takes place at Circuit Zandvoort on 23 August, the 36th time the circuit has hosted a Formula One World Championship round. It is the final scheduled Dutch Grand Prix, as the promoter elected against extending the contract beyond 2026.

### Sprint format

The Dutch Grand Prix is the fifth of six sprint-format events on the 2026 calendar and marks the first time the Dutch Grand Prix has featured the sprint format.

### Previous race

Lando Norris claimed victory at the Hungarian Grand Prix on 26 July, with Max Verstappen finishing second and championship leader Andrea Kimi Antonelli taking third. Charles Leclerc and Lewis Hamilton rounded out the top five.

### Championship standings

Antonelli heads into Zandvoort with a commanding lead of 50 points over Hamilton, who sits second on 169 points. Russell is close behind in third on 160 points, keeping the Mercedes intra-team battle tight. Leclerc holds fourth for Ferrari on 138 points, while Norris's Hungarian victory has lifted him to fifth on 128 points, closing the gap to the leading group. In the constructors' championship, Mercedes leads on 379 points, well clear of Ferrari on 307 and McLaren on 220, with Red Bull fourth on 177 points.

### Practice

The sole free practice session was held on 21 August, with Antonelli setting the pace for Mercedes ahead of Norris and Russell.
