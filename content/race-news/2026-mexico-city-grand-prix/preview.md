The 2026 Mexico City Grand Prix is the nineteenth round of the championship and takes place at the Autódromo Hermanos Rodríguez over the weekend of 30 October–1 November. It is the sixth event held under the Mexico City Grand Prix name.

### Championship standings

Andrea Kimi Antonelli leads the drivers’ championship with 365 points, 123 ahead of George Russell. Lewis Hamilton is one point behind Russell, with Max Verstappen five points further back and Charles Leclerc three behind Verstappen. Mercedes leads the constructors’ standings by 133 points over Ferrari; Red Bull and McLaren are tied for third, 119 points behind Ferrari.

### Previous race

At the United States Grand Prix on 25 October, Antonelli finished first, ahead of Leclerc and Verstappen. Hamilton took fourth, with Lando Norris fifth.
