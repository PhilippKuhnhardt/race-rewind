### The race

The 2026 Canadian Grand Prix is the fifth round of the 2026 Formula One World Championship and the 55th time the Canadian Grand Prix has featured as a round of the championship. The event takes place at the Circuit Gilles Villeneuve on Île Notre-Dame in Montreal across the weekend of 22–24 May, marking the 45th time the circuit has hosted the event.

### Sprint format

The Canadian Grand Prix is the third of six rounds in the 2026 season to use the sprint format, and marks the first time the Canadian Grand Prix has featured the sprint format.

### Weather

Conditions at the circuit are expected to be cloudy.

### Previous race

At the Miami Grand Prix on 3 May 2026, the race start was brought forward from 16:00 to 13:00 local time due to the threat of heavy rainstorms. Andrea Kimi Antonelli took victory over the 57-lap distance, with Lando Norris and Oscar Piastri completing the podium. George Russell finished fourth and Max Verstappen fifth. After the race, Verstappen received a five-second penalty for crossing the pit lane exit line following his pit stop, while Charles Leclerc, who had crossed the line sixth on track, was handed a drive-through penalty converted to 20 seconds for leaving the track and gaining an advantage on multiple occasions, dropping him to eighth in the final classification.

Leclerc sits third on 59 points, while Norris and Hamilton are level on 51 points in fourth and fifth, separated only by countback.
