### Event

The United States Grand Prix is the 18th round of the 2026 championship and the 47th running of the event as a world championship round. It takes place at the Circuit of the Americas in Austin, which hosts the Grand Prix for the 14th time.

### Championship standings

Andrea Kimi Antonelli leads the drivers’ standings by 102 points over George Russell. Lewis Hamilton, Max Verstappen and Charles Leclerc are clustered behind Russell, separated by 14 points from third to fifth. Mercedes leads Ferrari by 134 points in the constructors’ standings, while McLaren is just five points ahead of Red Bull.

### Previous race

At the Singapore Grand Prix, Max Verstappen finished ahead of Charles Leclerc and Antonelli. Lando Norris and Oscar Piastri completed the top five.
