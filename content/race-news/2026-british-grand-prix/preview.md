### The race

The 2026 British Grand Prix is the ninth round of the 2026 Formula One World Championship and the 77th time the British Grand Prix has featured as a round of the world championship. The event takes place at the Silverstone Circuit in Northamptonshire over the weekend of 3–5 July, marking the 61st time the circuit has hosted the event. Silverstone previously hosted the 70th Anniversary Grand Prix in 2020.

### Sprint format

The British Grand Prix is the fourth of six rounds in the 2026 season to use the sprint format, and the second British Grand Prix to feature it.

### Previous race

At the Austrian Grand Prix on 28 June 2026, George Russell took victory at the Red Bull Ring across 71 laps in sunny conditions. Max Verstappen finished second, with championship leader Andrea Kimi Antonelli third. Oscar Piastri and Lewis Hamilton rounded out the top five.

### Championship standings

Entering Silverstone, Andrea Kimi Antonelli leads the Drivers' Championship on 171 points, with a 40-point advantage over team-mate George Russell in second. Lewis Hamilton sits third on 125 points, keeping the fight within Mercedes. Oscar Piastri and Lando Norris occupy fourth and fifth for McLaren, separated by just one point. In the Constructors' Championship, Mercedes holds a commanding lead on 302 points, with Ferrari second on 204 and McLaren third on 159. Red Bull sit fourth on 115 points, while Alpine occupy fifth on 57 points.

### Practice

The sole free practice session was held on 3 July 2026 at 12:30 local time. Lewis Hamilton set the fastest time for Ferrari, with Kimi Antonelli second for Mercedes and Charles Leclerc third for Ferrari.
