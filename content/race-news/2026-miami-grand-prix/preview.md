### The race
The 2026 Miami Grand Prix is the fourth round of the 2026 Formula One World Championship and the fifth running of the Miami Grand Prix at the Miami International Autodrome in Miami Gardens, Florida. The event takes place across the weekend of May 1–3.

### Sprint format
Miami features the sprint format for the third time in the event's history and is the second of six sprint-format rounds on the 2026 calendar.

### Between-race developments
The Bahrain and Saudi Arabian Grands Prix, originally scheduled to immediately precede Miami, were cancelled due to the Iran war. The resulting gap in the calendar was used to introduce changes to the technical regulations, primarily concerning the power unit. Following Oliver Bearman's crash at the Japanese Grand Prix, the FIA held a series of meetings with power unit developers, team representatives, Formula One Management, and manufacturers on April 9, 15, 16, and 20 to evaluate refinements to the electrical energy deployment rules. One proposed solution involved reducing the rate of electrical energy deployment while increasing the amount that can be harvested during "super clipping" — a phenomenon where the battery charges on full throttle against the engine. The FIA confirmed that these regulation changes would be introduced for the Miami Grand Prix. As a result of the rule changes implemented during the April break, the sole free practice session was extended from 60 to 90 minutes.

### Previous race
At the Japanese Grand Prix on March 29, 2026, the race start was delayed by ten minutes to 14:10 local time following an accident in the Porsche Carrera Cup Japan support series that required barrier repairs at turn 12. Over 53 laps, Andrea Kimi Antonelli took victory ahead of Oscar Piastri and Charles Leclerc, with George Russell fourth and Lando Norris fifth.

### Championship standings
Mercedes hold a commanding lead in the Constructors' Championship with 135 points, well clear of Ferrari on 90 and McLaren on 46. In the Drivers' Championship, Mercedes teammates Andrea Kimi Antonelli and George Russell occupy the top two positions with 72 and 63 points respectively, giving the Silver Arrows a strong grip on both titles through the opening three rounds. Charles Leclerc sits third on 49 points, keeping Ferrari in contention, while Lewis Hamilton is fourth on 41 points. Lando Norris rounds out the top five on 25 points.

### Practice
The sole free practice session on May 1 was topped by Charles Leclerc for Ferrari, with Max Verstappen second for Red Bull and Oscar Piastri third for McLaren.
