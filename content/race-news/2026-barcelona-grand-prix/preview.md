### Background

The 2026 Barcelona-Catalunya Grand Prix takes place at the Circuit de Barcelona-Catalunya in Montmeló across the weekend of 12–14 June 2026. It is the seventh round of the 2026 Formula One World Championship and marks the first running of the event under the Barcelona-Catalunya Grand Prix name. The circuit previously hosted thirty-five editions of the Spanish Grand Prix, making this its 36th Formula One event in total.

The renaming comes as Madrid's new Madring circuit takes over the Spanish Grand Prix name for its 2026 debut, meaning Spain hosts two Formula One rounds in the same season for the first time since 2012, when the European Grand Prix was held at the Valencia Street Circuit. The Circuit de Barcelona-Catalunya has also secured a multi-year extension that places the Barcelona-Catalunya Grand Prix on the calendar in 2028, 2030, and 2032, rotating with the Belgian Grand Prix, which appears in alternate years.

### Previous race

At the Monaco Grand Prix on 7 June 2026, Andrea Kimi Antonelli took victory ahead of Lewis Hamilton over 78 laps. The race result was subject to significant post-race revision. Sergio Pérez received a ten-second time penalty for having his front-right wheel outside the starting box at a standing restart following a red flag, dropping him from tenth to fifteenth and costing Cadillac what would have been its first Formula One point. Fernando Alonso was promoted to tenth, giving Aston Martin its first point of the season.

More significantly, Alpine successfully petitioned for a Right of Review of two five-second pit lane speeding penalties that had been imposed on Pierre Gasly during the race. On 12 June, the stewards rescinded both penalties after determining that the applied distance used to calculate pit lane speed differed from the theoretical shortest distance, and that Gasly's actual speeds of 58.7 km/h and 58.8 km/h were below the 60 km/h limit. Gasly was reinstated to third place, with Isack Hadjar moving to fourth, Oscar Piastri to fifth, and Racing Bulls drivers Liam Lawson and Arvid Lindblad to sixth and seventh respectively. The decision prompted McLaren and Red Bull to notify their intention to appeal, while Mercedes explored legal options given that George Russell had served a related penalty during the race that could not be retrospectively removed.

### Between-race developments

The amended Monaco classification remains subject to any appeal lodged by McLaren and Red Bull following the reinstatement of Gasly's podium finish.

### Championship standings

Andrea Kimi Antonelli leads the Drivers' Championship on 156 points, a commanding margin over his Mercedes teammates Lewis Hamilton (90 points) and George Russell (88 points). Charles Leclerc sits fourth on 75 points, with Oscar Piastri fifth on 58 points. Mercedes dominates the Constructors' Championship on 244 points, well clear of Ferrari on 165 points and McLaren on 116 points. Red Bull occupy fourth on 69 points, with Alpine fifth on 50 points.

### Practice

George Russell set the pace in both the first and third free practice sessions, leading Oscar Piastri and Charles Leclerc on each occasion. Lando Norris topped the second session ahead of Russell and Piastri. The third session was interrupted by a red flag after Valtteri Bottas lost his brakes and ended up in the gravel at turn 10.
