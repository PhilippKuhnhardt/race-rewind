### Background

The 2026 Belgian Grand Prix is held at the Circuit de Spa-Francorchamps in Stavelot, taking place across the weekend of 17–19 July. It is the tenth round of the 2026 Formula One World Championship and the 71st running of the Belgian Grand Prix as a round of the Formula One World Championship. The event marks the 59th time the championship has visited the Spa-Francorchamps circuit.

### Previous race

At the British Grand Prix on 5 July 2026, Charles Leclerc took victory at the head of a Ferrari-led charge, with George Russell crossing the line second and Lewis Hamilton third. Lando Norris finished fourth, while Isack Hadjar rounded out the top five. The race ran for 52 laps under partly cloudy skies.

### Championship standings

Andrea Kimi Antonelli leads the Drivers' Championship on 179 points, holding a commanding advantage over Mercedes teammate George Russell on 136 points. Lewis Hamilton sits third on 132 points, keeping the fight within the same team at the top. Lando Norris and Charles Leclerc are separated by just two points in fourth and fifth on 85 and 83 points respectively. In the Constructors' Championship, Mercedes dominate on 315 points, with Ferrari second on 215 points and McLaren third on 167 points. Red Bull sit fourth on 118 points, while Alpine occupy fifth on 57 points.

### Practice

Three free practice sessions were held ahead of the race. Max Verstappen set the pace in the opening session on 17 July, with Lewis Hamilton and Charles Leclerc completing the top three. Antonelli moved to the front in the second session later that day, ahead of Norris and Verstappen. Antonelli again topped the third free practice session on 18 July, with Norris second and Verstappen third. The session was notable for Hamilton crashing late on, breaking his right-rear suspension.
