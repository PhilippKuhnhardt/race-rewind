### The race

The 2026 Italian Grand Prix is the thirteenth round of the 2026 Formula One World Championship, held at the Monza Circuit in Monza across the weekend of 4–6 September. It marks the 76th time the event has been staged at Monza and the 77th running of the Italian Grand Prix as a round of the World Championship. The race is scheduled for 6 September 2026.

### Championship standings

Andrea Kimi Antonelli leads the Drivers' Championship on 242 points, with a commanding 59-point advantage over second place. George Russell and Lewis Hamilton are locked together on 183 points, separated only by countback, while Lando Norris sits fourth on 159 points and Charles Leclerc fifth on 155 points. In the Constructors' Championship, Mercedes lead convincingly on 425 points, with Ferrari second on 338 points and McLaren third on 265 points. Red Bull occupy fourth on 189 points, ahead of RB F1 Team on 70 points.

### Previous race

At the Dutch Grand Prix on 23 August 2026, held under sunny skies over 72 laps, Lando Norris took victory ahead of Antonelli and Russell. Hamilton crossed the line fourth with Leclerc fifth. The result allowed Norris to close the gap to Antonelli at the top of the standings, while Russell and Hamilton maintained their joint hold on second place.

### Practice

Three free practice sessions took place ahead of the race. In the opening session on 4 September, Charles Leclerc set the pace for Ferrari ahead of his teammate Lewis Hamilton and George Russell. The second session later that day saw Russell go quickest, with Leclerc and Antonelli completing the top three. In the third and final practice session on 5 September, Russell again topped the timesheets, this time ahead of Hamilton and Max Verstappen.

Conditions at Monza are sunny for the race weekend.
