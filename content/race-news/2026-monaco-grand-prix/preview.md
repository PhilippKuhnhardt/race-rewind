### The race
The 2026 Monaco Grand Prix is the sixth round of the 2026 Formula One World Championship and the 72nd time the Monaco Grand Prix has featured as a round of the championship. The event takes place at the Circuit de Monaco across the weekend of 5–7 June, marking the 83rd time the circuit has hosted the event.

### Championship standings
Andrea Kimi Antonelli leads the Drivers' Championship on 131 points, a commanding margin over Mercedes teammate George Russell on 88 points. Charles Leclerc sits third on 75 points, with Lewis Hamilton close behind on 72 points. Lando Norris rounds out the top five on 58 points. In the Constructors' Championship, Mercedes hold a substantial lead on 219 points, with Ferrari second on 147 points and McLaren third on 106 points. Red Bull sit fourth on 57 points, ahead of Alpine on 35 points.

### Previous race
At the Canadian Grand Prix on 24 May 2026, Antonelli took victory to extend his championship lead. Hamilton crossed the line in second place, with Max Verstappen completing the podium in third. Leclerc finished fourth, while Isack Hadjar came home fifth, albeit a lap down. The race was shortened from its scheduled 70 laps after two additional formation laps were required following a mechanical issue for Arvid Lindblad of Racing Bulls.

### Practice
Three free practice sessions were held ahead of the Monaco Grand Prix. In the opening session on 5 June, Charles Leclerc set the pace for Ferrari, with Lewis Hamilton second and Max Verstappen third. The session was interrupted by two red flags — the first caused by Isack Hadjar crashing into the barrier at turn 14, and the second by debris from Fernando Alonso's Aston Martin late in the session. The second practice session, also held on 5 June, was led by Hamilton ahead of Leclerc and Verstappen. A red flag late in the session was triggered when Sergio Pérez's Cadillac suffered overheating brakes that caught fire. In the third and final practice session on 6 June, Antonelli topped the timesheets for Mercedes, with Leclerc and Hamilton second and third respectively. A red flag was shown after Oliver Bearman crashed his Haas into the wall at turn 9.
